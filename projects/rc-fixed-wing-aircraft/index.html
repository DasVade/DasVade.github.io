First-year undergraduate project

RC Fixed-Wing Aircraft

A hands-on aircraft build combining a 3D-printed fuselage, purchased propulsion and radio-control equipment, assembly and flight testing.

  • 3D printing
  • Airframe assembly
  • RC electronics
  • Flight testing

Airframe and component integration

The aircraft was built during my first undergraduate year. A 3D-printed fuselage housed the electronics and supported the assembled wing, tail and landing gear. The build integrated a purchased battery, electronic speed controller (ESC), motor, propeller and radio-control equipment.

Assembly connected the mechanical airframe to the electrical power and control chain: battery to ESC, ESC to motor, and radio commands to the onboard equipment.

Ground checks and flight testing

The aircraft successfully flew and was damaged during landing. The surviving recordings above capture ground testing; no flight recording survives.
