About

I am a MEAM 3+2 student at the University of Pennsylvania and a Mechanical Engineering student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. My research aims to bridge principled machine perception and reliable robotic deployment.

I focus on multi-object tracking, geometry-aware reconstruction, and world modeling, with applications to field robotics under occlusion, illumination variation, and sensing noise.
