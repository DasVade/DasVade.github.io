About

I am a MEAM 3+2 student at the University of Pennsylvania and a Mechanical Engineering student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I am interested in robust AI systems that transfer from controlled settings to real-world robotic platforms.

My work focuses on object detection and tracking, geometry-aware 3D reconstruction, and world modeling for long-horizon autonomy. I value methods that are both principled and deployment-ready.
