About

I am a MEAM 3+2 student at the University of Pennsylvania and a Mechanical Engineering student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My interests include robotic perception, multi-object tracking, and learning-based 3D reconstruction and world models, with emphasis on robustness and deployment.
